WELCOME TO RWAMUCUCU SEED SECONDARY SCHOOL

In active alignment with the new lower secondary curriculum framework, our sports and physical education program treats athletic talent as an essential academic and professional pathway. We provide structured training in compulsory physical education, track field athletics, and strategic indoor and outdoor disciplines. By embedding the core values of discipline, leadership, and emotional resilience into our training, we ensure that sports act as a vital tool for holistic personal growth, health management, and national pride, while intentionally scouting and nurturing the future champions of Uganda
